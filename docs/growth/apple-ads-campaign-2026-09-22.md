Search Match is disabled so Apple does not expand delivery beyond these explicitly selected search intents. This implements the Search Results, exact-match-first direction in the [marketing strategy](marketing.md#apple-ads-advanced), while remaining a single small campaign rather than the strategy's eventual multi-ad-group structure.

## Spend boundary and limitations

USD 6.67 per day is approximately USD 200 across this 30-day date window. It is **not** a hard monthly spend cap: Apple Ads describes the amount as a daily budget and notes that daily spending can vary. The campaign end date is therefore a second, time-based control, not a guarantee of an exact USD 200 charge.

The USD 0.25 maximum CPT is far below Apple's displayed suggested bid of USD 6.33 at campaign creation. This may protect the budget but can also result in little or no delivery. It is a deliberate test constraint, not evidence that the bid can win auctions or acquire profitable users.

No action should be taken on impressions, installs, CPA, or ROAS until Apple completes review and sufficient reporting time has elapsed. Any later change to budget, bid, keywords, placement, geography, or creative must be recorded separately with the observed account state and date.
