## Positioning

LinePaycheck is the worker's independent pay ledger: expected pay from actual recorded work and explicitly confirmed, supported rules, compared with reviewed paycheck facts.

It is not a comprehensive wage-law compliance audit, payroll processor, legal representative, union-endorsed product, tax estimator or guaranteed wage-recovery service. A source URL or worker confirmation does not establish expert verification or complete agreement coverage.

## Reusable copy

| Pro | Recurring audits of later paychecks; describe only features actually in the selected release. |
| Trial | Eligible annual customers receive the introductory offer actually supplied by StoreKit. Show the full renewal price and period. Monthly is immediately paid. |

Do not publish universal statutory weekly-overtime/regular-rate, every-state or full-CBA coverage. Do not advertise unimplemented travel/rest/meal/storm guarantees or named verified agreement packs. Keep this boundary even when a user leaves an unsupported-rule note blank. See [the legal control register](legal/README.md).

## Screenshots, demonstrations and endorsements

Use actual captures of the release-candidate UI, with consistent synthetic work/rates/dates and the same visible scope qualifiers as the product. Link each capture to its source/build and retain its checksum in the release-evidence record. Concept art is not proof of a shipping feature.

A hypothetical missing-pay example must say it is illustrative. A calculated difference is not a recovered-wages testimonial. A claim about an actual recovery needs the worker's permission and evidence of the correction/payment. Do not fabricate reviews, workers or typical savings, or condition rewards on positive reviews. Disclose material connections where applicable.

Accessibility and speed claims require the actual feature/device/test evidence; line coverage is not an accessibility or field-usability certification. Use away from active electrical work and driving; larger controls do not make the app electrical safety equipment.

## Acquisition plan retained, not spending approval

The earlier strategic preference remains a hypothesis: test a small, high-intent Apple Ads Search Results campaign before fragmenting spend across social and other ad networks. The archived draft preserves the keyword groups, proposed staged budget, annual/monthly mix and sensitivity calculations. No campaign, spend, customer outcome, commission-program eligibility or target conversion is established by that draft.

Before funding a campaign, verify current platform mechanics and benchmarks, actual publisher commission, supported keyword promise, selected binary, storefront availability, offer eligibility and measured cohort economics. Use the defined D30 contribution metric rather than calling it company or bank-cash profitability. Do not treat hypothetical targets as forecasts or silently add analytics/advertising SDKs to improve measurement.

## Approval and maintenance

Every public objective claim needs an accountable reviewer and a reference to shipping behavior, tests or other substantiation. The release preflight requires current store assets, policy/support snapshots and owner review. The local active-copy scanner flags common overclaims but is not a legal or semantic approval service.

Historical research remains historical. New wording belongs here, not in an unreviewed resurrection of archived slogans. Review new tracking, territories, named agreements or regulated services before changing the current local-first scope.
