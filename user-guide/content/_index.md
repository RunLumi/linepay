## Start with the facts. Keep the evidence.

LinePaycheck estimates pay from the work you record and the rules you confirm. On payday, it compares those records with the paycheck facts you have reviewed. It does not connect to your employer or decide what you are legally owed.

New here? Follow [your first pay period]({{< relref "/getting-started" >}}). Already have a paycheck? [Choose the matching work period and import it]({{< relref "/importing-paychecks" >}}). Moving phones? [Make a complete backup first]({{< relref "/backup-and-restore" >}}).

**Before any full-paycheck comparison:** record the complete period and confirm what the paystub’s gross amount includes. A partial work log, take-home deposit, or unconfirmed rule can make a convincing number misleading.
