## Start with Free

You can set up a pay profile, record work, inspect expected pay, and use the first comparable paycheck audit without creating a LinePaycheck account. Unknown gross basis or unconfirmed complete work does not consume that first comparable audit.

A free audit is not an Apple subscription trial. Continuing free does not itself start a recurring charge. Once the Free audit has been used, new recurring audits require verified Pro access; already saved work, audits, and exports remain accessible without Pro.

## Choose a billing option

Open **Settings → View Pro options**. The launch U.S. pricing is $9.99 monthly or $79.99 yearly. Prices, currency, eligibility, and applicable terms shown in the app and Apple’s purchase sheet are authoritative for your storefront and purchase.

The yearly amount is billed yearly; any monthly equivalent is explanatory, not a separate monthly charge. There is one Pro entitlement with two billing durations, not a credit balance or payment per scan.

An eligible annual offer can provide **seven days free**, followed by the displayed auto-renewing yearly price. Monthly is a paid alternative without that introductory trial. Do not assume a trial is available simply because Annual is selected: actual offer information and eligibility come from the App Store.

An audit performed while Pro or its verified trial is active does not consume an otherwise unused Free audit. Trial eligibility is separate and is not reset by reinstalling the app, deleting data, or choosing another duration in the same subscription group.

## Review renewal and cancel intentionally

Settings can show **Pro trial active**, **Pro active**, and a **Renews** or **Ends** date. Use **Manage subscription** to inspect the subscription in your Apple account and follow Apple’s cancellation controls.

For a trial you do not want to renew, follow the cancellation timing displayed with the offer; the app’s annual-trial notice asks you to cancel at least 24 hours before trial end. Verify cancellation and the displayed access-end date in Apple’s subscription screen. Deleting the LinePaycheck app or its local data does not cancel an Apple subscription.

A report of a pay difference is not a promise that the app will recover its purchase price.

## Restore purchase access

Choose **Settings → Restore Purchases** using the Apple account associated with the subscription. This checks purchase access; it does not restore pay data. Restore a [complete backup]({{< relref "/backup-and-restore" >}}) separately on a new phone.

Pending approval is not completed access. If prices cannot load or a purchase cannot be verified, read the message and retry appropriately. Do not repeatedly purchase merely to force an entitlement refresh.

## Expiration or billing problems

Apple billing-grace, retry, expiration, or revocation states can produce different access notices. Review payment and subscription details with Apple. Existing records remain readable; loss of Pro is not deletion of your data.

For account-specific billing or refund requests, use Apple’s support channels. For an app-access problem, [contact LinePaycheck support]({{< relref "/support" >}}) without sending payment credentials.
