## Work and rules

**Actual worked time:** elapsed time in the recorded shift after its recorded unpaid breaks. It is not increased to represent guaranteed pay.

**Base hourly rate:** the entered straight-time rate before configured multipliers. The current setup labels this rate in USD.

**Multiplier:** the factor applied to a base rate, such as 1.5× or 2×. Only enter an applicable, confirmed rule.

**Daily overtime tier:** a multiplier that starts after a configured number of worked hours on the payroll-local date. It is not the same as a weekly overtime rule.

**Callout minimum / guarantee:** a configured paid-hours entitlement for qualifying callout work. A derived top-up is separate from actual clock time.

**Flat per diem:** a configured allowance per worked local date. Its tax treatment and inclusion in paystub gross are not inferred by LinePaycheck.

**Payroll timezone:** the saved timezone used to interpret work dates and boundaries. It need not equal the phone’s current timezone.

**Clock-time review:** an explicit Repeat Shift step shown when a copied payroll-local time is ambiguous, nonexistent, or otherwise cannot be accepted automatically. Repeated times require choosing the actual occurrence; nonexistent times require manually entering and confirming the real work time.

**Rule snapshot / version:** the saved set of rules used for a calculation. Later profile changes must not silently replace the meaning of historical results.

**Source reference:** a title, link, or section you retained to explain a rule. It is not independent verification by the app.

## Paycheck comparisons

**Expected wages:** calculated wage components from the recorded work and supported rules; per diem is shown separately.

**Gross pay:** the source paycheck amount before deductions on the basis being confirmed. The app asks whether this figure includes its represented per diem.

**Net / take-home pay:** the payment after deductions and adjustments. Do not compare a bank deposit directly with expected wage gross.

**Current period versus year to date:** values for this paycheck’s covered period versus cumulative values for the year. Confirm current-period values for this audit.

**OCR:** on-device text recognition used to suggest values from a document. A suggestion is not a confirmed fact.

**Confirmed field:** a value you reviewed and explicitly confirmed. This establishes input status, not the legal correctness of the agreement.

**Comparison scope:** which totals or lines the app actually compared. Gross-only and confirmed-line comparisons provide different evidence.

**Possible difference:** expected minus confirmed paid on a comparable basis. Positive means expected was higher; negative means paid was higher.

**Not comparable:** the prerequisites do not support a full-paycheck comparison. It is not a zero difference or a clean match.

**Audit revision:** a saved record of an assessment’s work, rules, calculation, and confirmed facts at that time. Corrections preserve earlier revisions.

## Records and access

**Closed work period:** frozen work and rule context retained in History. Its paycheck can still be added later.

**Complete backup:** a manual restorable snapshot including saved data and retained original files. It is not live sync and is not password-encrypted by the app.

**Data export:** structured records or a report for another purpose. The JSON export and audit PDF are not the complete backup format.

**Restore Purchases:** refresh App Store entitlement access. It does not recover wage history.

**Free audit versus Pro trial:** the first comparable audit allowance is separate from Apple’s eligible, time-limited introductory subscription offer.
